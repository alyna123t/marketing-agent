## Voice
- Honest over polished. Show the losses. "Here's the scar" energy.
- Specific over sweeping. Real numbers, real trades, no vague hype.
- One reader per post. Don't write for traders and founders at once.

## Don't overclaim
- No forward-looking guarantees. No "best/only/guaranteed".
- Every claim needs evidence (data drop, shipped PR, or public source).
- Required caveats when results are shown: window, sample size, that losses are included.
