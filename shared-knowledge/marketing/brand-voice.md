# Brand voice — Simmer

*Read by `brand-voice-guard`. Built from Simmer's own past X posts, a competitor
comparison (Elastics), and current research on what stops the scroll. The monthly
retro updates this from what actually converted.*

---

## The one-line voice

**Honest, specific, and plain-spoken. Lead with the human outcome, back it with real numbers, and never hide the losses.** Simmer sounds like a sharp trader telling you the truth — not a product changelog, and not a hype account.

Two things are true at once and both matter:
- Simmer's **honesty is the moat** ("you can still lose," "dry-run is the default"). Keep it. No competitor sounds this straight.
- Simmer's posts currently **lose non-technical readers** to jargon and buried leads. Fix that without losing the honesty.

---

## The core problem to fix (diagnosis from our own posts)

Our posts are **feature-led and insider-coded**. Elastics' posts are **outcome-led and human-legible** — that's why they land with people who don't already know what an agent is.

| Ours (what to stop) | Why it loses people |
| --- | --- |
| "our **Player Goal Value Skill** likes Vinícius Júnior 1+ goals" | Names an internal mechanic no reader knows. Lead with the *bet and the edge*, not the skill's name. |
| "drop it on a **daily cron (after 02:00 UTC)**" | Ops jargon. A trader hears "set it and it runs." |
| "the **copyability screen** cuts **slippage** risk, not market risk" | Two insider terms in one line. Keep the honesty, lose the vocabulary. |
| "your agent gets the **runnable version**" | "Runnable" is a dev word. Say "your agent can actually trade it." |
| "Get excited for one of the most thrilling games…" | Generic hype opener. Says nothing only Simmer could say. |

**What Elastics does that we should steal (the frame, not the voice):**
- Leads with the **outcome in plain English**: "trade with words," "describe your thesis in plain English." We *have* this exact capability ("paste an X thread, your agent runs it") — we bury it in tweet 3. Put it first.
- Uses **legible proof**: "$2M pre-seed," named backers. Our version of legible proof is *real PnL including losses* — our data drop. Lead with the number.

**What we already do well and must keep:**
- "Polymarket just quietly shipped combo markets. **Parlays, for prediction markets.**" — timely hook + plain-English reframe of a complex thing. *This is our best post. Do more of this.*
- "**The honest part:** it auto-executes trades… you can still lose." — radical honesty as a hook. Keep it, and lead with it instead of burying it.

## How to write a hook (the first line does 90% of the work)

Readers scroll ~3–5 posts/second. The first line is the whole game; almost nobody reads line 2 if line 1 is weak. Every post leads with one of these, in Simmer's honest register:

- **The contrarian edge** — "The market says Japan hangs with Brazil. Our model disagrees on one bet:" *(our angle, stated as disagreement with the crowd)*
- **The honest part** — "The honest part: it auto-executes real trades. You can still lose." *(our signature — trust as a hook)*
- **The receipt** — "We ran 40 agents last week. Net: down 2%. Here's what the winners did differently." *(lead with the real number, losses in)*
- **The plain-English promise** — "Describe a trading strategy in a sentence. Your agent runs it. No code." *(Elastics' frame, our capability)*
- **The timely reframe** — "Polymarket just shipped combo markets. Parlays, for prediction markets. Here's the play." *(news + a plain-English translation)*

Rule: if the first line names an internal Simmer feature before it names a **reader outcome or a real number**, rewrite it.

## Before / after (using our real posts)

**1. World Cup bet post**
- Before: *"Get excited for one of the most thrilling games… our Player Goal Value Skill likes the Vinícius Júnior 1+ goals: 38.5¢. Use our pre-made skills…"*
- After: *"The market respects Japan. Our model likes one bet against the grain: Vinícius Júnior to score, priced at 38.5¢. Here's the read 🧵"*
- Why: contrarian edge first, plain language, price as proof, skill name dropped.

**2. Copytrader skill**
- Before: *"This skill copies the best World Cup traders on Polymarket for any agent. Auto-curated daily, no wallet list to pick."*
- After: *"Want to copy the best World Cup traders on Polymarket — without picking wallets or watching charts? Set it once, it runs the tournament. (Yes, it trades real money. Dry-run is the default.)"*
- Why: outcome + question first, honesty caveat kept, "cron/UTC" cut.

**3. The honest-part post** — already strong. Just promote it to the *lead* of its own post instead of tweet 4 of a thread.

## Banned / rewrite list (jargon that loses non-technical readers)

Cut or translate these unless the post is explicitly for the **builder** audience:

| Don't say | Say instead |
| --- | --- |
| "daily cron / after 02:00 UTC" | "runs every day on its own" |
| "runnable version / runnable skill" | "a strategy your agent can actually trade" |
| "copyability screen / slippage" | "we filter out trades you couldn't actually copy at a good price" |
| "guardrails / tune the guardrails" | "set your limits" |
| "RFQ system," "fully collateralized" | translate to the outcome, or cut |
| generic hype: "get excited," "thrilling," "game-changer," "revolutionary" | a specific claim or number |

Audience exception: for the **builder** audience (see `audiences.md`), technical terms are fine and expected. This list is about not defaulting to insider language for everyone.

## Don't overclaim (keep the honesty edge)

- No forward-looking guarantees. No "best," "guaranteed," "can't lose."
- Every claim maps to evidence — a data-drop line, a shipped feature, or a public source (`claim-evidence-linter` enforces).
- When results are shown, state the **window, the sample, and that losses are included**. The losses are not a disclaimer to minimize — they're the credibility.
- The trust line is a feature, not fine print: "it trades real money," "dry-run is the default," "you can still lose" belong *in* the post.

---

## Structure defaults

- One post, one idea, one audience (`audiences.md`).
- Hook first. Proof second. One CTA last (a tagged link).
- Numbers over adjectives. A real price or PnL beats "huge" every time.
- Threads: line 1 must stand alone and stop the scroll; one proof beat per post after.
- Visuals help (posts with images get materially more engagement) — pair receipts with a clean chart when possible.
